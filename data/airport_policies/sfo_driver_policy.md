# SFO Driver Policy

## Driver Queue

Drivers must remain in the designated airport staging queue while waiting for requests.

Drivers must not intentionally abandon the queue to obtain priority elsewhere.

Queue position must be maintained according to airport operating procedures.

## Driver Cancellation

Drivers should avoid cancelling accepted airport trips except when operationally necessary.

Repeated driver cancellations should be investigated by the operations team.

## Driver Incentives

Driver incentives may be considered when driver supply is insufficient.

Incentive amounts should be based on:

- Supply shortage
- Operational severity
- Expected duration of the disruption

High-value incentives require management approval.
