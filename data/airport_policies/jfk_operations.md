# JFK Airport Operations Policy

## Airport
John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

## General Operations

Drivers must use designated airport staging areas.

Drivers must follow airport pickup instructions and maintain their assigned queue position.

## Operational Thresholds

A completion rate above 83% is considered normal.

A completion rate below 83% requires investigation.

A completion rate below 74% is considered a significant anomaly.

## Queue Management

Drivers must not intentionally bypass the airport queue.

Operations teams should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Active drivers
- Request volume
