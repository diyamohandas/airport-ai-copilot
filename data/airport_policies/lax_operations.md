# LAX Airport Operations Policy

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## General Operations

Drivers must follow designated airport staging and pickup procedures.

Drivers must remain in the approved staging area while waiting for passenger requests.

## Operational Thresholds

A completion rate above 84% is considered normal.

A completion rate below 84% requires investigation.

A completion rate below 75% is considered a significant anomaly.

## Queue Management

Drivers must maintain their queue position.

Drivers must not bypass other drivers.

Operations teams should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Queue size
- Driver cancellations
- Active drivers
- Request volume
