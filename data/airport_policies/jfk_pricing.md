# JFK Airport Pricing Policy

## Airport
John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

## Surge Pricing

The normal operating range is 1.0x to 1.2x.

The maximum permitted surge multiplier is 1.5x.

Surge may be increased when passenger demand exceeds available supply.

## Approval Requirements

Surge increases require operational review.

A surge multiplier of 1.3x or greater requires explicit human approval.

The AI system must not execute high-risk surge changes without human approval.

Any recommendation above the maximum permitted multiplier must be blocked.
