# SFO Airport Pricing Policy

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Surge Pricing

The standard operating surge multiplier at SFO is between 1.0x and 1.2x.

The maximum permitted surge multiplier is 1.5x.

A surge multiplier above 1.5x is not permitted under normal operating conditions.

## Surge Increase

Increasing surge may be considered when:

- Completion rate falls below the operational threshold.
- Passenger request volume significantly exceeds available driver supply.
- Average ETA increases significantly.
- Airport queue conditions indicate insufficient driver availability.

## Approval Requirements

Any surge increase requires operational review.

A surge increase below 1.3x requires manager approval.

A surge increase of 1.3x or greater is classified as a high-risk action and requires explicit human approval before execution.

## Policy Enforcement

The AI system must never recommend or execute a surge multiplier above the maximum permitted value of 1.5x.

Any recommendation exceeding 1.5x must be blocked.
