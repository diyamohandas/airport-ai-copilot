# LAX Airport Pricing Policy

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## Surge Pricing

The normal surge range is 1.0x to 1.2x.

The maximum permitted surge multiplier is 1.6x.

Surge increases should be considered when demand significantly exceeds available driver supply.

## Approval

Surge increases require operational review.

A surge multiplier of 1.3x or greater requires explicit human approval before execution.

The AI system must not execute a surge increase without the required approval.

Any surge recommendation above 1.6x must be blocked.
