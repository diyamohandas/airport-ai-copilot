# SFO Airport Operations Policy

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## General Operations

Drivers operating at SFO must follow designated airport pickup and staging procedures.

Drivers must remain within designated staging areas while waiting for passenger requests.

Drivers must not leave the airport queue after entering the designated queue unless they have accepted a passenger request or are instructed by airport operations.

## Queue Rules

The SFO driver queue operates on a first-in, first-out basis.

Drivers must maintain their position in the queue.

Queue jumping or bypassing other drivers is prohibited.

## Operational Thresholds

A completion rate above 85% is considered normal operational performance.

A completion rate below 85% should be investigated.

A completion rate below 75% is considered a significant operational anomaly.

## Monitoring

Operations teams should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Active driver count
- Request volume
- Surge multiplier
